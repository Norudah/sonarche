# A library that files itself, and lets you fix it

1.0 could download and file. This release is about everything that comes **after**: taking back a badly identified album, moving a track to the record it belongs to, giving artists a face, and making playlists.

## Filing by hand when the machine gets it wrong

- A track can be moved to another album, and the move can be undone.
- A record can say it is a collection rather than an album, which switches off the complaints that do not apply to it.
- Genres go into the families you decide, by dragging a chip onto a family card.
- A whole import can be taken back out of the ark from the history.

## Playlists

Playlists, favourites, a cover per playlist, an order you rearrange by dragging. They are mirrored as `.m3u8` in the library folder, for players outside Sonarche.

## Covers and portraits

- A cover can be replaced from a file, a pasted link or the clipboard, and recropped without being replaced.
- Every artist can wear an image of their own, set the same way.

## The player

Audio now runs through a native engine: queue, shuffle, repeat, gapless playback, and the system's media keys answer. Lyrics show on demand.

## Under the hood

A library of several thousand tracks no longer bogs the lists down: only the visible rows really exist. Launch got lighter, and downloaded files are repaired on arrival — the ones showing `0:00` everywhere else play properly again.
